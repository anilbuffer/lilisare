# General Guidelines
- Ensure all new changes follow a clear and well-structured Information Architecture (IA). Every page should be logically connected with a consistent user workflow, maintaining seamless navigation across the entire platform. Avoid duplicate pages, redundant user flows, or overlapping functionality to ensure a cohesive and intuitive user experience.
- Strictly adhere to the **LiliSare – Agency Admin Workflow** defined in [RULES.md](file:///e:/lilisare/RULES.md). Do not introduce features, pages, or logic outside the scope of the 10 defined journey stages (Intake & Referral, Client Registration, Caregiver Registration, Documents & Onboarding, Case Manager & Authorisation, Caregiver Approval & Activation, Scheduling & EVV, Care Notes & Clinical Monitoring, Compliance Management, Billing & Payroll).
- Anchor all product design in the **Three Connected Journeys** (Client Journey, Caregiver Journey, Agency Operations Journey) as defined in [RULES.md](file:///e:/lilisare/RULES.md#L96). Do not build isolated department silos; views and workflows must show how progress in one journey impacts the connected journeys within a household.
- **Navigation:** Navigate newly built pages from dedicated sidebar menu items with clear, dedicated names matching existing sidebar styling and role-based navigation.
- **Modals vs. Full Pages:** Avoid long, content-heavy modals; implement extensive views (such as multi-step creation wizards, detailed profiles, and comprehensive record editors) as dedicated full pages.
- **Design Consistency:** Maintain uniform styling and layouts across all UI elements (cards, typography, action links/buttons, tables, filters, and flyout drawers) in line with existing LiliSare pages.
- **Brand & Domain Terminology:** Align all copy, service names, sample data, and tone with the LiliSare brand, incorporating clear terminology for Patients/Clients, Caregivers, and Households.
- **Role-Dedicated Routing Architecture:** All routing URLs MUST strictly include the role-dedicated prefix matching the user persona (e.g. `/intake/...`, `/admin/...`, `/hr/...`, `/scheduler/...`, `/billing/...`, `/clinical/...`, `/compliance/...`, `/portal/...`, `/caregiver/...`). Specifically, core entities must always be accessed via their role prefix (e.g. `/intake/caregivers/`, `/intake/clients/`, `/intake/households/` for Intake Coordinator, and `/admin/caregivers/`, `/admin/clients/`, `/admin/households/` for Agency Admin). Never introduce un-prefixed or ambiguous root entity URLs. All future pages, sub-routes, and navigation links must strictly adhere to this role-scoped convention.
- **Global UI Standardization (Cards, Modals & Forms):**
  - **Global Card Outlines:** All cards across the entire application must strictly apply uniform card outline styles matching `/intake/reports/`: `bg-white rounded-xl lg:rounded-2xl border border-slate-200 shadow-2xs hover:border-[#3D6B22]/50 hover:shadow-md transition-all duration-200 overflow-hidden`.
  - **Global Modal & Form Layouts/Styles:** Standardize all modal and form layouts/styles globally to match `/intake/pipeline/new/` and the shared components in `@/components/ui/Modal` and `@/components/ui/FormControls`:
    - **Modal Shell & Backdrop:** Container: `relative w-full bg-white rounded-2xl sm:rounded-[20px] shadow-[0_20px_50px_rgba(0,0,0,0.15)] border border-slate-200 overflow-hidden flex flex-col`. Backdrop: `fixed inset-0 bg-slate-900/60 backdrop-blur-xs z-50 flex items-center justify-center p-3 sm:p-4`.
    - **Modal Header:** `border-b border-slate-100 p-4 sm:p-5 flex items-center justify-between bg-white` with emerald icon badge `w-10 h-10 rounded-xl bg-[#3D6B22]/10 border border-[#3D6B22]/20 text-[#3D6B22]`, title `text-base sm:text-lg font-bold text-slate-900 tracking-tight`, description `text-xs text-slate-500 font-medium mt-0.5`, close button `p-2 rounded-xl text-slate-400 hover:text-slate-700 hover:bg-slate-100 transition-colors`.
    - **Modal Body & Form Inputs:** Inputs: `w-full px-3.5 py-2.5 bg-slate-50/50 border border-slate-200 rounded-xl text-sm focus:bg-white focus:outline-none focus:ring-2 focus:ring-[#3D6B22]/20 focus:border-[#3D6B22] shadow-[0_2px_8px_rgba(0,0,0,0.02)] transition-all placeholder:text-slate-400 text-slate-800`. Labels: `text-[13px] font-medium text-slate-700 block mb-1.5`. Section headers: `text-[11px] font-semibold text-slate-400 uppercase tracking-wider mb-2 flex items-center gap-2` with divider line `<div className="h-px bg-slate-100 flex-1"></div>`.
    - **Modal & Form Action Buttons:** Primary button: `inline-flex items-center justify-center gap-2 px-5 py-2.5 text-xs sm:text-sm font-bold text-white bg-[#3D6B22] hover:bg-[#345C1D] rounded-xl shadow-md shadow-[#3D6B22]/20 transition-all active:scale-95 cursor-pointer`. Secondary / Cancel button: `inline-flex items-center justify-center gap-1.5 px-4 py-2.5 text-xs sm:text-sm font-semibold text-slate-700 bg-white hover:bg-slate-50 border border-slate-200 rounded-xl transition-all shadow-2xs hover:text-slate-900 cursor-pointer`.
  - **Common Sidebar and Header Integration:** Every portal section—including the Agency Admin Portal (`/admin/...`), Intake Portal (`/intake/...`), and department workspaces—MUST be wrapped in `<AppLayout>` with the unified sidebar and TopBar header. Never omit portal root layouts.
